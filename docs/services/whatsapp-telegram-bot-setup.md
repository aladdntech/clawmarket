# WhatsApp / Telegram Bot Setup

## Service Overview
A custom AI‑powered chatbot for WhatsApp or Telegram that answers common questions, handles greetings, and escalates to a human when needed. Built for businesses that want better response times and 24/7 availability.

## Deliverables
- Custom AI chatbot on WhatsApp or Telegram
- Trained on your FAQ/knowledge base
- Auto‑replies for business hours, greetings, and common questions
- Human handoff capability
- Basic dashboard for monitoring conversations
- Deployment and connection to your number/bot
- Documentation + 30‑minute training session
- 30 days post‑delivery support

## Customer Input Required
- WhatsApp Business number **or** Telegram bot token
- FAQ/knowledge base (PDF, DOC, or text)
- Business hours + greeting messages
- Escalation contact details (for human handoff)
- Brand tone (formal/casual/friendly)

## Timeline
**30–60 minutes** of AI setup once all inputs are received.
- **AI Generation Time:** 30–60 minutes (training + build)
- **Customer Review Time:** testing with your team
- **External Wait Time:** platform approvals/outages if applicable

## Process
1. **Intake form** and kickoff to gather requirements
2. **Knowledge base training** (minutes)
3. **Bot build** (30–60 minutes)
4. **Testing** with sample conversations
5. **Deployment** to WhatsApp or Telegram
6. **Training session** and handoff
