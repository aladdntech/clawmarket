### 3) **Panama (Sociedad Anónima)**
**Formation costs (gov + agent):**
- Provider package (includes government fees + registered agent + compliance): **USD 3,000 (incorporation + 1st year)**. Source: mirrasia.com.
- Annual renewal from year 2: **USD 2,500**. Source: mirrasia.com.

**Timeline:** **6–10 business days** (plus courier/apostille/banking time). Source: mirrasia.com.

**Required client info/documents:**
- Company names, shareholder & director details (passport + address), company purpose, resident agent appointment, KYC/AML docs. Source: mirrasia.com.

**Privacy / nominees:** Not explicitly stated. (UBO registry is private per source.)

**Banking options:** Optional EMI account opening **USD 400**; Panama local bank **USD 2,000**; Puerto Rico CBI account **USD 3,880**. Source: mirrasia.com.

**Tax implications:** Territorial tax system: foreign-source income generally not taxed locally. Source: mirrasia.com.

**Annual maintenance costs:** Annual renewal **USD 2,500**; annual government levy **B/.300 (Tasa Única)**. Source: mirrasia.com.

**Pros/Cons:**
- **Pros:** Territorial tax system; clear legal framework; defined timeline. (mirrasia.com)
- **Cons:** Requires resident agent + UBO registration obligations. (mirrasia.com)

## General Process (Step‑by‑Step) — Verified Items Only
Steps vary by jurisdiction, but sources consistently show the following when documented:
1. **Choose a company name** (Panama, Estonia). [mirrasia.com, eestifirma.ee]
2. **Provide shareholder/director details + KYC/AML documents** (Panama). [mirrasia.com]
3. **Appoint a resident agent / registered agent** where required (Panama). [mirrasia.com]
4. **Submit the incorporation filing** and **pay government fees** (Estonia state fee, Delaware/others per fee schedules). [eestifirma.ee, corpfiles.delaware.gov]
5. **Receive incorporation/registration confirmation** (Estonia typically 1 business day once filed). [eestifirma.ee]
6. **Ongoing compliance**: annual fees or taxes (e.g., Delaware LLC $300 annual tax; Seychelles annual fee; Panama annual levy). [corp.delaware.gov, seychellestrusts.com, mirrasia.com]
