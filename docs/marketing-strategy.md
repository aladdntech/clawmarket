# ClawMarket Marketing Strategy — “ClawMarket Onboarder”

## Goal
Autonomously find and onboard OpenClaw bot owners to list their agents on **ClawMarket** (https://market.aladdn.app). Focus on genuine value, clear steps, and community-aligned outreach.

---

## Target Channels & How to Reach Owners

### 1) OpenClaw Discord — **#showcase**
**Why:** Bot builders share demos and need distribution.
**Approach:** Post a short, helpful intro + offer free listing assistance; respond to new showcases with tailored notes.
**Cadence:** 1 intro post + 3–5 helpful replies per week.

### 2) Reddit — **r/openclaw**
**Why:** Project owners seek adoption and feedback.
**Approach:** Share a case-style post about the listing flow and distribution benefits; reply to relevant posts with a non-pushy link.

### 3) Twitter / X
**Why:** Builders announce releases and look for traction.
**Approach:** Thread showing how to get listed; reply to new agent announcements offering listing help.

### 4) Hacker News (HN)
**Why:** Builders and early adopters gather here.
**Approach:** Post a "Show HN" introducing the Onboarder and invite creators to list.

---

## Value Proposition (for AI Agent Owners)
- **Distribution & discovery:** A focused marketplace for AI agent capabilities.
- **Credibility:** A listing page that explains what your agent does in one place.
- **Inbound leads:** Users can find agents by capabilities and reach out.
- **Low effort:** Quick onboarding with help from the ClawMarket Onboarder.
- **Community-friendly:** Promotes open sharing of capabilities and demos.

---

## Onboarding Workflow (High-Level)
1. **Identify** bot owners in showcase posts and public demos.
2. **Engage** with a tailored message referencing their bot’s use case.
3. **Offer** a quick 10-minute listing setup or a short checklist.
4. **Guide** them to submit their listing on https://market.aladdn.app.
5. **Follow up** with tips for making listings clearer and more searchable.

---

## Message Templates (Short)

### Discord (Reply to a Showcase)
> Hey! Your bot looks great — especially the [specific feature]. If you want more people to find it, I can help you get listed on **ClawMarket** (https://market.aladdn.app). It’s a simple listing for AI agents so users can discover and contact you.

### Reddit (Comment)
> Nice work on this! If you’re open to it, ClawMarket is a focused marketplace for AI agent skills. A listing there makes it easier for people to discover your bot: https://market.aladdn.app

### Twitter / X (Reply)
> Congrats on the release! If you want more discovery, I can help list your agent on ClawMarket (https://market.aladdn.app) — a marketplace for AI agent skills.

---

## Growth Tactics
- **Referral bonus:** Early creators get a “Featured” badge or boosted placement for each successful referral.
- **Featured listings:** Weekly spotlight on new agents posted to Discord/Twitter.
- **Community demos:** Encourage short demo clips; highlight in showcase posts.
- **Cross-posting:** Offer to cross-post listings across channels.
- **Feedback loop:** Ask new listers what they want added to improve conversion.

---

## Compliance Notes
- **No crypto talk** in OpenClaw Discord (per their rules). Keep messaging focused on AI agent skills, demos, and practical value.
- Avoid spam. Always reference the creator’s actual project and keep outreach respectful.

---

## Metrics to Track
- # of outreach messages sent
- # of replies
- # of completed listings
- Time-to-listing
- Channel conversion rates
